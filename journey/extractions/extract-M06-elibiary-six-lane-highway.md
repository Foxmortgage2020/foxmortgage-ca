# M-06: Wally Elibiary, the six lane highway and the data bank
source: How_to_Fund__100__Million_a_year_and_work_24_hours_a_week_-__mp3.txt
context: US loan officer, Dallas Fort Worth, licensed 1999, about 200 million a year with six staff on a 24 hour week. Manifest guessed Renee Huss, corrected. Promotional episode, he sells a book and an academy Peckford links.

## Big idea
Stop only receiving realtor referrals. Become the hub that sends referrals out to wealth professionals, then market to their databases as your own.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] Built the team rather than bought it, six people with no mortgage experience. Annuity comp pays 5 basis points of team production at three years and 10 at five, his golden handcuffs.
- [POST-FUND] Concierge sequence after closing: 7 day, 30 day, and 6 month calls, an annual review, and an efficiency checkup, each with a set purpose and script.
- [REFER] Refers business out to CPAs, advisors, will attorneys and insurance agents, then tracks returns.
- [REFER] Every October he asks each advisor their income goal and average commission, then computes the monthly referrals needed to top their list.
- [LEAD] Inserted himself into the advisor's annual review, which already collects paystubs, by adding a mortgage statement request returned as a cost analysis.
- [RENEW] Trigger monitoring across the data bank: a listing, another lender's credit pull, or heavy new card debt. Each trigger routes back to the advisor for a warm introduction.

## Gates
- Mega agents only, 50 to 100 million producers. Solo agents declined.
- To be a partner's top source you must send referrals, not only receive.
- Stay in relationship after funding or the servicer keeps the client.

## Numbers
36 million in 2013 rising to about 200 million [stated]. Six staff, 24 working hours a week, 20 vacation weeks [stated]. Refers out over 2 million in commissions a year since 2021 [stated]. Database 4,000 to 28,000 households in ten years [stated]. Mix about 40% realtors, 30 to 35% database, 25% wealth partners [stated].

## Delegation map
Six loan officers carry the files. He calls 95% of his time air game, spent on systems and people rather than in the file.

## Solo translation
The concierge call ladder is a sequence engine needing only a trigger per interval. Sales Boomerang is the Opportunities and Radar engine already built, Homebot is OwnWell, Jungo is Zoho. What does not automate is the commitment to feed partners first.

## Tensions
US market, so no break penalties and no Canadian renewal cycle. Peckford counters that Canadian lenders are strong at retention. He refers out as the engine while most episodes chase referrals in. The 24 hour week rests on six staff, not a solo result.
