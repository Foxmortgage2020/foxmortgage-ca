# M-18: Geri Janes, hiring late and the Google review engine
source: When_Success_Steals_Your_Freedom_with_Geri_Janes_mp3.txt
context: Canadian broker, licensed 2006, Ontario mortgage agent level two at BRX, transcript spells it Jerry. Team of three, a director of operations and two document specialists. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
Success removed the freedom she entered the business for, and the fix is hiring earlier than feels ready plus a client experience run by templates and video.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] The Google review ask rides the document checklist and repeats at broker complete, closing day and post closing. Megan asks even callers who do not proceed.
- [INTAKE] A 30 minute discovery call sets timeline, purpose, income, down payment and credit, and decides fit. Private files are referred out.
- [DOCS] Finmo carries the application and the document list. Blue marks it sent and emails a reminder every five days until the package lands.
- [PLAN] She underwrites, then a Loom walks the client through the application and lenders. Existing clients can thumbs up straight to submission instead of the strategy meeting.
- [COMMIT-TO-FUND] Approval flips Blue, which assigns a document specialist, auto tasks the signing package and checklist, then tasks her own review before any client email.
- [MODEL] Files route to the two document specialists by strength and closing speed, and they touch nothing before commitment. Megan owns new inquiries, underwriting and training.

## Gates
- No near full document package, no strategy meeting. She names employment letter, pay stubs, T4s.
- No approval, no document specialist on the file.
- No client thumbs up, no signing package.
- No principal review, no approval email to the client.

## Numbers
Just over 350 Google reviews [stated], and she closes 75 to 80% of Google sourced files [stated]. About 20 to 25% of clients asked leave one [stated]. Megan carries 20 to 25% of files end to end [stated]. Ownwell claims up to 60% open rates [self-reported, sponsor sells it].

## Delegation map
She keeps the discovery call, underwriting, video reviews, strategy meetings and submission. Megan takes new inquiries, training and a quarter of files. A contractor built the Blue templates.

## Solo translation
Most of this automates. Blue tasks and Finmo reminders do the document chase, so the specialist role is package assembly a tool could template. The videos and the fit call stay human. The director of operations is the seat automation does not fill.

## Tensions
She demands almost a full document package before a strategy meeting, rejecting the least resistance school. She hired document specialists who start at commitment, not Atkin's front to back underwriter. Her regret is hiring late, against the wait until ready default.
