# M-13: Taylor Atkins, the leads already in the filing cabinet
source: ILMB-P-0172_2.txt
context: Kelowna BC broker, Canadian, about 100 files funded last year, one team member. Episode three of a Peckford coaching series. Identity corrected, Taylor Atkins not Atkinson. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
The best untapped lead source is data you already hold, and what converts it is a broker with an opinion reaching out personally.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] Backfill the monitoring platform from files already on hand, statements from applicants who never funded plus non-subject properties overlooked.
- [LEAD] Rank monitored clients by interest saved net of penalty, then email roughly the top 20 personally. Not a report, an opinionated recommendation.
- [LEAD] Sponsor mechanic worth stealing, two triggers: savings net of penalty, and engagement, where clients lingering in their monthly report get flagged even when the math looks neutral.
- [LEAD] Mailout to monitored clients naming BRX's 2,500 dollar monthly referral draw. Reported as a miss, not smoothed over.
- [RENEW] Renewal calls get triaged for pain points first, debt restructure, longer amortisation, equity takeout. If the bank offer wins he says take it, then asks for the confirmation to load them into monitoring.
- [REFER] Clone the top realtor partner by asking for introductions to agents they already refer to in other markets.

## Gates
- No statement, no monitoring. The renewal call does not end without the confirmation to load.
- No shiny channel without a proven operator.
- No stopping at one referral partner. Onboard four to get one.
- No AI script goes out unreviewed.

## Numbers
7.4 million funded at 10 March, gross 65,500, net 42,888 [stated]. 188 hours, 363 dollars per hour [stated]. 58 monitoring seats in 40 days, 53 from old data [stated]. 140 mailed, 2 referrals, under 1% [stated]. 44,000 dollars extra in one month from proactive follow-up [self-reported by the sponsor's founder, who sells the feature].

## Delegation map
Him: budgets and Looms, renewal triage calls, the personalised savings emails, and partner development, which he says he dislikes. Team member: document collection and labelling, now AI-assisted.

## Solo translation
This is the Radar thesis stated out loud, including the failure. The 53-seat backfill is a one-time data project a solo agent can run from stored statements, and the ranking and draft email automate cleanly. The opinion must stay human, since information alone did not move people.

## Tensions
M-01's summary says Taylor kills renewals entirely, and he does not. He takes about five renewal calls a month and converts them into a monitoring signup. The sponsor's own claim cuts against full automation: automate detection, keep the human recommendation.
