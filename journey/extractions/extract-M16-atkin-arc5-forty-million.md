# M-16: Nate, 40 million pipeline and the bottleneck that moved
source: Monday_Episode_07-06-269vybm.txt
context: Episode five of the Nate series, an Ontario BRX mortgage agent, Canadian. Five months in, first hire landing. No surname or episode number is stated, so the manifest guess of Atkin and Ep 728 is unconfirmed. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
"The bottleneck doesn't go away, it just moves." Once the first hire absorbs underwriting, the constraint jumps outside the factory to lead generation.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] The bottleneck test is rerun each session, if workload tripled tomorrow where does it break first. The first answer was underwriting.
- [MODEL] The hire transitions gradually while still employed elsewhere, with systems and tech walked through before day one. SOPs go into Scribe so hire two is cheaper.
- [REFER] Clone the top partner by asking each consistent realtor who covers their listings on vacation, and who they refer to elsewhere, then request the intro.
- [REFER] Lunch and learns solicited proactively to realtor teams and brokerages, using the time the hire buys back.
- [LEAD] Joint client-facing webinar with a realtor partner on a live question, like selling a Toronto condo. The realtor invites their own database.
- [RENEW] The database is layer two, not one. Grow the client base first, then hire someone whose only job is pulling deals out of it.

## Gates
- No new referral channel until the existing 10 realtors have been asked for intros.
- No niche added while one-to-many is unworked.
- "You can't be a secret agent." If the audience does not know you sell mortgages, it is not lead gen.
- No hire starts cold.

## Numbers
First week of June: 13 million funded, 3 million broker complete, 8 million applications received, 12 million pre-approval, near 40 million by mid-June [stated]. 80% of business from realtor partnerships, 20% renewals and client referrals [stated]. 10 consistent partners plus 10 loose, the top 10 about 50% of pipeline [stated].

## Delegation map
Nate: every file until the hire starts, all partner relationships, all speaking. His son, a BRX agent in his office, interrupts through door taps. The hire takes underwriting, and a database miner is the named future hire.

## Solo translation
Hire one is underwriting, which automation already absorbs, so a solo agent skips it and keeps the four core relationships human. The clone-the-partner ask and webinar cadence are CRM and calendar triggers, not headcount.
