# M-04: Meredith Kaminski, trigger boards and process sheets
source: How_Changing_Your_Business_Model_Can_Improve_Your_Business_-_mp3.txt
context: Winnipeg broker, One Link, residential since 2008. Four people: her, partner Dave, an associate, an assistant. OwnWell sponsor read at the open, incentive noted: monthly equity and buying-power monitoring, high open rate.

## Big idea
The market is rarely the problem. Trigger-driven boards that treat every client identically let four people carry volume without dropping work.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] Five Kanban boards: leads, pre-approval, active deal, anniversary, renewal. Moving a card fires triggers into a named person's lane.
- [LEAD] Pre-lead trigger. A referred name who never calls gets chased, then the realtor is asked why.
- [COMMIT-TO-FUND] Triggers confirm lawyer instructions are correct, and follow up the client before and after funding.
- [COMMIT-TO-FUND] Google review request fires at approval, before packing starts, when the client is happiest.
- [INTAKE, SIGN] Per-stage client process sheets, with separate closing sheets for purchase, refinance, transfer, and lawyer files.
- [RENEW] Assistant reaches out at 9 months by email and mail, again at 6 and 3 months. Replies get calls.
- [RENEW] The call is arithmetic, not reassurance. Balance, amortization, and payment worked to size the real increase.
- [RENEW, PLAN] Discovery: plans for the house, a move inside two to three years, debt, renovations, cottage, rentals. A short horizon moves advice off a five year fixed.
- [POST-FUND, REFER] Term-keyed database plan, newsletter and themed contest every couple of months. Referrals thanked by printed card plus a Tree Canada donation.

## Gates
- One process for every client. No exception for size, family, or friends.
- No last document, no advance. Income exceptions are common.
- Heavy debt at credit check disqualifies a transfer, it becomes a refinance.
- No switch unless it adds value. If the current lender is competitive, say stay.

## Numbers
75% from past clients and referrals [stated]. Purchases 25 to 35%, rest refinances and transfers, more refis than transfers [stated]. Renewal touches at 9, 6, 3 months [stated]. Four staff, three in office [stated]. Contest prize 100 dollars [stated].

## Delegation map
Partners: referrals in, underwriting, submission, every advice conversation. Associate: application processing, documents, signing packages, funding conditions, closeout. Assistant: renewal outreach, anniversary letters, admin. Principal minutes not stated.

## Solo translation
The assistant is almost fully automatable, the 9, 6, and 3 month touches and anniversary letters are a sequence engine on the book. Process sheets are static per-stage artifacts a portal serves by file type. The renewal math conversation stays human, it is where the refinance is found.
