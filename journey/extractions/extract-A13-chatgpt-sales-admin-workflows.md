# A-13: Five ChatGPT workflows for sales admin
source: Sales_Reps_Are_Wasting_2_Hours_a_Day_Without_These_AI_Workfl_mp3.txt
context: Manifest guess confirmed. US enterprise account executive, not Canadian. Sells a waitlist for Rhythm AI, a sales copilot, and an AI Sales Accelerator of 30 plus custom GPTs.

## Big idea
Not a better prompt, but a purpose-built assistant per task, pre-loaded with your own templates and required fields.

## Patterns
- CODIFIED EXTRACTION: transcript → assistant holding your own required fields and email templates → CRM fields, follow-up email, calendar invite → human copies and sends. Field list is yours, not a generic framework.
- CONFIDENCE GAPS: transcript → extraction → output names what it could not find → human knows what to ask next call. Missing data surfaced, never invented.
- PER-ACCOUNT MEMORY: all correspondence for one account → dedicated project → catch-me-up paragraph on demand. Replaces jumping across four systems.
- PROMPT AMPLIFICATION: one-line ask → prompt builder → long structured research prompt → deep web research → human probes and demands citations. First answer is a start, not the deliverable.
- TEMPLATE SKELETON: fixed slide-by-slide structure plus transcripts → 80 to 85 percent draft → human finishes it.
- VOICE REHEARSAL: scenario and counterpart profile → spoken back-and-forth with feedback → practice before real call.

## Human-in-the-loop
Every workflow. Nothing sends itself. He copies into the CRM, fixes formatting, and says do not use it word for word. The gate is the template library built first.

## Stack mentioned
ChatGPT custom GPTs, Gemini Gems, Claude, PromptCowboy, Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong. Fox equivalents: Claude Code skills per task, Zoho CRM, deal room for per-account memory, native render engine.

## Claims
- Saves two hours a day, work "as good or better" than manual [self-reported, selling a copilot waitlist and GPT bundle].
- Call workflow returns an hour, runs in 10 to 20 seconds [self-reported, same incentive].
- Slide drafts land 80 to 85 percent complete [self-reported, same incentive].

## Failure modes
Consistent output "takes a little bit of know how". Uploading every email and transcript is "very painful". Image generation is not good enough for slides. Early research read "bland and generic". Drafts need formatting fixed.

## Compliance flags
He warns twice to check company policy before uploading transcripts, emails, or private information. Client conversation data into a third-party model is the exposure. One example requests named individuals' LinkedIn profiles by city. FSRA: drafting is fine, presenting stays with Mike.

## Fox fit
Warm book, not cold, so it transfers. Strongest at INTAKE and PLAN: codified extraction is the Ask Fox call review shape, confidence-gap flagging matches the honest not-captured rule. Per-account memory is the deal room. Template skeletons fit lender notes and client presentations.
