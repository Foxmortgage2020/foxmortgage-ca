# M-12: Nate Atkin, the bottleneck is the broker
source: ILMB-P-0169.txt
context: Episode three of the Nate Atkin series, recorded 18 March. Canadian, Hummingbird Mortgages, effectively a one man shop with one part-time fulfilment helper. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
A broker who is his own production line grows only by buying time back in stages before the first hire lands, keeping just the part clients pay for.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] Morning touch points to realtor partners before any file is opened, Dustin Woodhouse 10-at-10 style, daily. Fires and active files come second.
- [MODEL] Time blocks with no gaps, workout then office then lead gen then files. Emotion is not an input: "If it's on the list, it gets done."
- [INTAKE] The discovery call is the one step he keeps, "Nate 101 massive value." Peckford's rule is paint the face, hand off the background.
- [MODEL] Every handoff introduces the specialist as better than the broker at that job. Never the word assistant.
- [MODEL] Daily 15-minute pipeline review. Staff name three must-dos and the principal corrects sequencing before a missed appraisal kills a financing condition.
- [MODEL] A bridge hire comes before the real hire, contract per-file admin for compliance and signing packages, bought to free time to train the underwriter.

## Gates
- No lead gen done, no file work touched.
- No confirmed entity and payroll structure from the CPA, no hire.
- No bonus below a minimum target profit threshold. The company gets paid first.
- No handoff without an introduction that elevates the receiver.

## Numbers
Pipeline: 6 million closed, 2.5 million approved, 1.1 million broker complete, 11.1 million pre-approvals, 9 million applications received, with his own qualifier that the last two may not go anywhere [stated]. Offloadable share of his workload, his estimate, 60% [stated]. Peckford's ramp 20, then 30, then 40 [stated].

## Delegation map
Him: lead gen, discovery, documents, files, submissions. One helper on "a little bit of fulfilment." Peckford's fix is to record every call so AI summaries carry context. Minutes per file not stated.

## Solo translation
The face-and-background split is the transferable rule, and automation replaces the background. The underwriter role is lender-side navigation and condition chasing, the fulfilment role is document collection, both partly automatable. The pipeline review is a report a system produces without a meeting.

## Tensions
Arc episode 3 of 5, diagnosing the bottleneck before the hire lands. Chad Wilson runs 300 files on three staff by gating work onto the client, while Nate has no client-side gates. Peckford argues software before headcount, and Nate's answer is a salaried human.
