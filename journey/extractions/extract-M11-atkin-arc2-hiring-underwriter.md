# M-11: Nate Atkin, hiring the underwriter
source: ILMB-P-0158_Audio6xe6z.txt
context: Episode two of the Nate Atkin series, recorded 21 January. Mostly Peckford coaching on sourcing, interviewing and paying a first underwriter. OwnWell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
The constraint is time, not leads, and the first real hire is an underwriter who carries a file front to back, not a helper who nurses approved files.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] Sourcing runs three ways: internal brokerage resources, lender underwriters who have hinted they want out, and Indeed. LinkedIn is deliberately skipped.
- [MODEL] Job postings carry an extra step, a form or short quiz, so resume spammers filter themselves out.
- [MODEL] Interview questions are grouped into six categories: underwriting experience, deal protection, capacity, systems, client interaction, and ownership and values.
- [MODEL] Answers must be specific to a named file. The tells are we versus I language, and vagueness about how a decline was reversed, which usually means the broker solved it.
- [MODEL] The calibration question on any volume claim is where in the file you pick it up, because someone doing only signing packages can quote a large number.
- [MODEL] Claimed tech proficiency is tested live by screen share. Peckford once hired on association alone and let the person go the next day.

## Gates
- No completed application step, no resume review.
- No live tech demonstration, no proficiency claim accepted.
- No shared contractor if you intend to scale. The person eventually goes exclusive with one broker.
- No piecework while dictating hours, because that reads as employment to the tax authority.

## Numbers
Pipeline 20 days into the year: 1.2 million broker complete, 800,000 submitted, 4.9 million in pre-approvals, 3 million in applications received [stated]. Salary 70,000 to 80,000 with a path toward 100,000 through bonus [stated]. An HR contractor for posting and first interviews at about 1,500 dollars [stated].

## Delegation map
Bonus is recommended on file count or company revenue rather than volume, because volume incentivises ignoring small clients and file count alone can incentivise rate buydowns. Open-book profit sharing is the model offered.

## Solo translation
The six interview categories double as a specification for what any replacement must cover, and the pick-up-point question is the right question to ask of an automation. The friction step in the posting is the same filter Tourloukis applies with a credit card.

## Tensions
Arc episode 2 of 5 in the Nate Atkin series. He is buying a human for the work the automation thesis says software should absorb. Peckford also tells him to skip fulfilment help and go straight to an underwriter, against the piecemeal path most brokers take.
