# M-09: Jim Tourloukis, the thirteen month renewal play
source: ILMB-P-0061.txt
context: Replay of a November 2022 conversation, same Ontario broker as M-08, in a rising rate market. Peckford promotes a paid webinar and coaching list, incentive noted.

## Big idea
Contact a fixed-rate client thirteen months before renewal so the transfer executes at nine months less a day, when the penalty is three months interest, not interest rate differential.

## Journey practices
- [RENEW] At 13 months he holds a rate for four months, then transfers at nine months less a day, because most incumbent lenders carry a six month product capping the penalty at three months interest. On a two year term the arithmetic moves the call to 11 months.
- [RENEW] Thirteen named campaigns run yearly, and a client is contacted only when one finds value, by his POP test: pain points, opportunity to save, protect the client.
- [RENEW] For variable clients he queries anyone priced worse than market, such as three or more years left at prime minus 40. The system computes each net saving, so the opening line carries a dollar figure.
- [INTAKE] A fifteen minute conversation moving the client from unconsciously to consciously incompetent. He asks what five things besides rate matter, and most go blank.
- [POST-FUND] Positions himself as the client's mortgage mechanic for twenty years.

## Gates
- Fixed clients only. The play does not apply to variable.
- No six month product at the incumbent lender, no play.
- Rising rates only. He states it fails in a flat or falling market.
- No contact without value.
- No realtors, as in M-08.

## Numbers
About 90% success on the play in that rising market [stated]. Files closing at 3.5% against a market of 5.5 to 5.7%, sized as 100,000 dollars over five years on a million dollar mortgage [stated]. Mix 30% purchases, 40% refis, 30% renewals [stated]. Two thirds from campaigns and past clients [stated]. 85 to 90% of clients variable [stated].

## Delegation map
He tells new agents to price their own hour, 250 dollars at half a million a year, and refuse any task hireable for less. First to go is document collection, then fulfilment.

## Solo translation
The 13 campaigns with a computed net saving per client is the Opportunities engine. The 13 month play is date arithmetic against maturity, and the extra input is whether the incumbent lender offers a six month product.

## Tensions
He dismisses CRM touching as worthless here, having described his own CRM touching clients 8 to 10 times a year in M-08. The play is bound to rising rates. Renewals are his engine, against Atkinson killing them.
