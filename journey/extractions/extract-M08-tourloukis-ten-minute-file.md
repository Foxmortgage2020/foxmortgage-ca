# M-08: Jim Tourloukis, 800 deals at ten minutes a file
source: ILMB_Live_8__How_Jim_Tourloukis_Handles_800_Deals_A_Year_mp3.txt
context: Ontario broker with Verico, 14 years in, formerly a mutual fund CFO. About 750 to 800 loans a year with two staff, at roughly ten broker minutes per file.

## Big idea
Volume comes from disqualifying fast and never chasing, so the only minutes spent go to files that will close.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] Sells B2B, landing corporate contracts with associations, employers and a relocation firm rather than courting individuals.
- [INTAKE] A seven to ten minute call, always him, interviewing the client rather than selling. He opens on the existing mortgage, lender, term position and rate, and says up front he wants direct answers.
- [DOCS] A fit gets one email with the application and document list, then silence. Nothing is chased. The appraisal credit card is taken up front, even where he intends to pay, because refusing it signals the client is not proceeding.
- [SUBMIT] He forwards the completed email to staff and exits. Appraisal is ordered immediately with a rush against the financing waiver date, giving a Monday to Friday cycle.

- [RENEW] A hand-built spreadsheet holds 9,000 past clients with term, rate and a computed opportunity value, so a campaign is a sort. Live example was clients on a ten year at 3.99.
- [RENEW] Speculative rate holds for clients coming due months out, then a call comparing the held rate against market and exit cost.

## Gates
- No fit in ten minutes, no file.
- No documents and no credit card, no advance, and no chasing.
- No realtors. He will not arrive hat in hand.
- No face-to-face meetings. Clients who insist go to a competitor.
- No lender that blocks future client contact.

## Numbers
750 to 800 loans a year at about ten broker minutes each [stated]. 92% funding ratio once the application is back [stated]. About 30 calls and 200 emails a day converting to roughly three deals, about 10% of calls [stated]. 9,000 past clients touched 8 to 10 times a year [stated]. About 60% of business from database second effort [stated].

## Delegation map
Him: the qualifying call, opportunity calls, campaigns, rate holds. Two staff: everything after the forwarded email, through to signing.

## Solo translation
The spreadsheet computing an opportunity value per client is the Opportunities engine, and he confirms it works while calling his version manual. What cannot be automated is the ten minute disqualification.

## Tensions
Refuses realtors outright, against every other file treating them as a channel. Deletes the face-to-face meeting, where Chad Wilson keeps a planning conversation as his conversion engine. He concedes this loses deals.
