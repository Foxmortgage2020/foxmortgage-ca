# M-10: Nate Atkin, the baseline before the first hire
source: ILMB-P-0153_audio.txt
context: BRX broker, about 18 months in, 38 to 40 million last year, targeting 80 million in 2026. Also owns an aluminum window company. Episode one of a monthly coaching series.

## Big idea
Treat the practice as a business rather than a job, which means protecting a daily lead generation block and hiring before it feels affordable.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] The first block of every day, ninety minutes to two hours from eight, is reach-out only. Calls, DMs and realtor contact, never files. Social posts are scheduled separately.
- [LEAD] Partner nurture runs on four-slide presentations, Loom videos and a weekly Friday value video, on a consistent cadence.
- [INTAKE] Fifteen minute discovery calls are batch-scheduled into one block, to avoid switching gears between file work and selling.
- [MODEL] Days are fixed at the front: wake around five, gym fasted with everything prepared the night before, office by half seven, then the reach-out block.
- [MODEL] The next hire is a full-time underwriter carrying a file front to back, on salary rather than per file, so attention is not split across other brokers.
- [MODEL] He interviews by asking how a candidate would handle specific files, treating learning something from the answer as the pass condition.

## Gates
- The morning reach-out block is protected. Small fires wait, and only a closing tomorrow interrupts.
- Salary, not piecework, or the arrangement becomes a bottleneck when he outgrows it.
- Do not hire a second version of yourself, and do not hire just to hit the deadline.

## Numbers
Fewer than ten files funded before joining BRX [stated]. 12 million in pipeline after 120 days [stated]. 38 to 40 million last year, goal 80 million [stated]. Reach-out block 90 to 120 minutes daily [stated]. Discovery calls 15 minutes [stated]. Hiring deadline 31 March [stated].

## Delegation map
He still does nearly everything himself. His stated zone of genius is communication and sales. Nothing is delegated yet, the problem the series opens on.

## Solo translation
The protected reach-out block and batched discovery calls are calendar discipline, not software. The underwriter he is hiring is the role automation targets, document collection and front-to-back processing. His admitted gap, never tracking what partners send back, is a two-way referral ledger and pure CRM work.
