# M-14: Taylor Atkins, hard files as the referral engine
source: ILMB-P-0178.txt
context: Identity corrected, the host says Taylor Atkins, not Atkinson. No episode number stated. Kelowna BC broker, Canadian, 8.9 million funded gross year to date. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
The files other brokers screen out are the fastest way to earn loyal referral partners, and a phone call beats a broadcast to a list that never opted in.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] Takes the deals others refuse, Poly B on inspection, GST on new builds, business-for-self, rentals in a corp by trust agreement.
- [INTAKE] Application and documents are done before his call, then one combined discovery and strategy call, always him. Cat runs fulfilment and underwriting.
- [MODEL] The next hire is a second Cat, not an underwriter, so the client talks to two people rather than a chain of four roles.
- [REFER] After a hard file closes he messages the buying agent, "I hope I kind of proved myself." Reviews and small gifts follow, with no ask attached.
- [REFER] Asks partners and old friends for intros. One produced a realtor doing 100 ends a year with no mortgage partner.
- [RENEW] Renewal-call segue, if I cannot beat your lender send it over anyway, then load the client into the monitoring tool.

## Gates
- No completed application and documents, no discovery call.
- No handoff chain. As few people between broker and client as possible.
- No underwriter-only hire. The next role does both start to finish.
- No coverage, no time off. A day rate is paid before either person leaves.

## Delegation map
Him: hard-file structuring, lender workarounds, the discovery and strategy call, and every referral partner conversation. Cat: fulfilment and underwriting end to end. Files sat five or six days on his desk in the busy stretch, triggering the second-hire thought.

## Solo translation
Cat's role is the one that does not automate, because nuanced underwriting is judgment, so automation replaces the coordination and status layer rather than the adjudication. The referral mechanics translate as triggered sequences, a post-close agent thank-you and a renewal-window prompt.
