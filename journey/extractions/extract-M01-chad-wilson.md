# M-01: Chad Wilson, 300 files with a team of three
source: How_Chad_Wilson_Closes_300_Files_a_Year_With_a_Team_of_Three_mp3.txt
context: Winnipeg broker, 23 years in. 250-300 files a year with a team of three: him, one staffer on compliance, one on renewal follow-ups. 10,000-client database in a smaller-mortgage market.

## Big idea
Volume without headcount comes from gates that push work onto the client, a database mined on triggers, and human time reserved for one high-trust planning conversation.

## Journey practices
- [INTAKE] Five-minute scripted first call, always him: who I am, how I operate, my promise, what to expect. Then the ball is in the client's court.
- [INTAKE, DOCS] Two-link email after the call. Link 1: application, consent, smart-doc upload. Link 2: book a 15-minute review the moment docs are submitted. Whole loop can finish within an hour.
- [PLAN] Calendly planning session offered from onset, 15-minute and 45-minute options. 75-80% book one. The 45-minute session is the conversion engine: business cycle, fixed versus variable, decisions pre-made so the file moves fast later.
- [SUBMIT] Accepted offer gets a response within one hour, always. Pre-approval letter tells the realtor to send the offer and MLS listing.
- [COMMIT-TO-FUND] Next-steps email fires the minute the commitment lands. Includes the manual MPP form for advance review plus insurance links.
- [COMMIT-TO-FUND] Proactive call and doc copy to the lawyer's office, sets expectations, asks them to call him with any questions so nothing spoils the client experience.
- [SIGN] Signing package with a Calendly signature appointment, zoom, phone, or in person. 30% book it, 70% just click-sign, and he calls the click-signers to confirm they understood what they signed.
- [RENEW] Monthly staff check-in through the renewal window. Planning session with him before the final decision.
- [RENEW] Straight renewals he can't beat get sent back to their bank armed with rate intel. Generates referrals through transparency.
- [REFER] Ask at two moments: the straight-renewal sendback and pre-signing when the client confirms they're happy. Script: have you been happy, then any friends or family who'd find value.
- [POST-FUND] Canadian Mortgage App install at pre-approval with the client's scenario preloaded. Hundreds of installs, cuts email, carries referrals.
- [LEAD, RENEW] Database mining: homebuilt CRM (Daylight) plus Zapier pulls plus Mailchimp sends. Point-in-time trigger campaigns, like the TD no-IRD window. Send once, no follow-up chase.

## Gates
- No pre-qualifications. We do approvals. Scares off clients he didn't want anyway.
- No documents, no answers, no advance.
- No chasing, ever. Clients who won't jump the hoops self-select out.
- Complete file to lender in exchange for fast turnaround. Funding ratio never below 90%.

## Delegation map
Him: intake call, planning sessions, opportunity calls, lawyer calls. Staff: one only compliance, one only renewal follow-ups. Tools: CRM and Zapier find, Mailchimp sends, Calendly books, smart docs collect, the app self-serves scenarios. Admits he doesn't measure campaign results.

## Solo translation
His two staff map one to one onto Mike's B3 compliance automation and B1 Radar plus sequences. The two-link email is Finmo application plus doc request plus booking link. The planning session is the one thing that can't be automated and it's where the 40% lives. The Radar's job is to book that conversation, not replace it.

## Tensions
Peckford's 711 says database-and-wait is broken. Chad doesn't wait, he mines on triggers. Taylor Atkinson kills renewals entirely. Chad admits weak measurement of his own campaigns.
