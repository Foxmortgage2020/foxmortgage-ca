# M-15: Nate Atkin, why cutting your own grass slows the business
source: ILMB-P-0180_1.txt
context: Episode four of the Nate Atkin series, Canadian, Hummingbird Mortgages, BRX. About 36 million pipeline in under six months, no staff yet, first hire signed and not started. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
Buy back the low-value hours and outsource the hiring process itself, because a broker hour beats every hour spent doing someone else's job.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] An HR firm runs hiring. It whittles the funnel, he reviews recorded Zoom interviews, then interviews finalists himself.
- [MODEL] The first hire is an underwriter with about 17 years of files, fluent in his tech stack, starting part time on mock files.
- [MODEL] She drafts the SOPs and he edits to finalise. Scribe screen-records a task and builds the document, so procedures exist before hire two.
- [MODEL] The proposed second hire is a local EA, not offshore, covering inbox, calendar and instant lead callback. VAs stay task specific and offshore.
- [MODEL] BRM structure over an assembly line. One person owns the file with the broker and is the referral partner's single contact.
- [LEAD] Lead plan once time is freed: realtors already strong, financial advisors parked, database monitoring, plus lunch and learns.

## Gates
- No customer journey mapped, no hire. A kitchen with no recipes.
- No prompt response, no good candidate. Same rule for leads and tenants.
- No extra body between broker and file owner. One person signs off.
- No DIY contracts. Province rules differ, so the HR contractor papers it.

## Numbers
Pipeline off his CRM: 9 million funded closed, 5.4 million broker complete, 12 million applications pending, 6 million received, 9 million pre-approvals, stated total about 36 million [stated]. The parts sum to 41.4 million [implied]. Landscaping 120 dollars a week against two to three of his own hours [stated]. Document work predicted to fall to 10 to 20% [stated forecast].

## Delegation map
Him now: file work, lead response, calendar, hiring. Going to the underwriter: file work and some email. Proposed for the EA: calendar, inbox, first lead contact, roughly 80% work and 20% personal.

## Solo translation
The EA half is replaceable now, because instant callback, booking, calendar defence and document chasing are sequences plus a booking link, not a salary. The underwriter half is not, and his own reason is the tell.

## Tensions
Arc episode 4 of 5, and the hire is signed but not started, so every delegation claim here is plan rather than result. Common advice hires an underwriter first, while the EA is argued as cheaper capacity per dollar.
