# A-15: SaaStr swapped ten sales humans for 20 agents
source: We_replaced_our_sales_team_with_20_AI_agents_here_s_what_hap_mp3.txt
context: Jason Lemkin of SaaStr, US not Canada, on Lenny's podcast. He sells sponsorships, tickets, a founder community, and AI go-to-market consulting.

## Big idea
Agents beat the mid-pack rep by cloning your best rep's script and working leads humans never answered.

## Patterns
- REACTIVATION: leads a rep skipped → agent re-contacts → human closes. The hand was already raised.
- INSTANT QUALIFICATION: enquiry → agent qualifies and books, beating a two-day callback.
- BEST-REP CLONING: top rep's real copy → template → agent A/B tests variants. The bar is the mediocre human.
- CORRECTION LOOP: ingest docs → agent drafts → human corrects hourly for 30 days → tuning compresses into one portable prompt.
- BASE SEGMENTATION: one list → exclusive segments per agent, or "it just becomes a mess."
- INCOGNITO SELF-AUDIT: anonymous email → walk your own funnel → worst moment becomes agent one.

## Human-in-the-loop
A chief AI officer reviews output 10 to 15 hours weekly, so this is not full automation. One human still closes. "Disappear, you will have zero roi."

## Stack mentioned
Delphi clone → Ask Fox on Claude. Artisan, Qualified and Agentforce → n8n plus Claude over the Zoho book. Momentum and Attention → Zoho. Sierra and Fin → Next.js portal. Replit → Claude Code. Reve → native render engine.

## Claims
[self-reported, he sells the community and the consulting]
- 1.2 humans plus 20 agents matches 8 to 10 GTM humans, productivity "not better, it's not worse".
- A general agent closed a $70k sponsorship alone.
- 70% response rate on leads sales had discarded.
- About 60,000 outbound emails, "pretty high rates", no figure.

## Failure modes
- "They don't work out of the box." Vendors "kind of lied" in 2024.
- It gave people wrong event dates, fixed an hour each morning.
- Hallucinations persist, so QA is permanent, never a launch task.
- Oversight costs 10 to 15 hours weekly, "agents work all night".
- Ceiling at 20 agents. Business process change is the limit, not software.
- Roughly 50k plus 25k for deployment. Nothing auto-trains.
- Output flat, only efficiency rose. He is "working the hardest I've ever worked".

## Compliance flags
Disclosure was dropped. They labelled digital Jason, then stopped, "we just send it". He concedes phone and SMS consent rules, and that "startups are breaking the rules". The whole CRM base uploads to third-party vendors, plus visitor tracking.

## Fox fit
Reactivation transfers cleanest to RENEW and REFER over the warm SMM base and lapsed files, where consent is held. Instant qualification fits LEAD and INTAKE. Volume outbound is cold-only, park it.
