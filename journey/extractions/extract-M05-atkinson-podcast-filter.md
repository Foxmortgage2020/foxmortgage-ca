# M-05: Taylor Atkinson, the podcast that killed the discovery call
source: How_This_Broker_Killed_Discovery_Calls__Boosted_Trust____Clo_mp3.txt
context: Kelowna broker, Venture Mortgages, funding since 2021. Book is high net worth and business-for-self. Two people, him and a business partner. OwnWell sponsor read, incentive noted, claims 60 to 80% open rates.

## Big idea
A long-running podcast builds enough trust that clients file an application and send documents before the first call, deleting the discovery call.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] Co-hosted local real estate podcast. Guests carry it, he speaks about eight minutes a show.
- [INTAKE] Application link goes out before any conversation, to protect his time.
- [DOCS] Partner collects documents and cleans the application, then he reviews it.
- [PLAN] The strategy call is the one client conversation he keeps. He walks fixed terms against variable and explains lender incentives, including that banks earn more on a five year.
- [SUBMIT] He underwrites to commitment, then hands back for fulfilment.
- [RENEW] Turns away renewals with no pain point, coaching the client on their existing lender and drafting an email to leverage against their bank.
- [RENEW] Keeps turned-away names and re-engages when an opening appears, such as a Scotia quick-close special at 3.99.
- [REFER] Guests include lawyers and appraisers, who he says help faster on a file.

## Gates
- No application, no call. Documents arrive before he speaks to anyone.
- No switch unless a pain point exists the current lender cannot solve.
- Never claim a rate you cannot beat. That bet kills referrals.
- Only files where he adds value. Insured rate battles declined.

## Delegation map
Him: strategy call, underwriting, commitment. Partner: documents, application cleanup, DocuSign and signing packages, fulfilment to close. He took a week offline while she ran it.

## Solo translation
The application-before-the-call gate needs no staff, only a link and enough trust to justify asking. His partner is a document and fulfilment engine, which is Finmo plus a doc-request loop and a signing-package skill. The podcast does not automate.

## Tensions
Against Chad Wilson and the point-guard model, both of which protect a discovery call. He deletes it. Calls the renewal gold rush overblown, against Kaminski running renewals hard.
