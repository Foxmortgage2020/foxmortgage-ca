# M-20: Your database is a referral engine
source: You_Don_t_Have_a_Lead_Problem_You_Have_a_Business_Model_Problem-esv2-speech-100pbtos7.txt
context: Confirmed Peckford solo, Canadian, BRX Mortgage founder and coach, 18 years in the business. No episode number is stated, so the Ep 711 guess is unverified.

## Big idea
Renewal-driven database marketing has stopped paying, so the database becomes a referral machine for wealth partners, and mortgages arrive as a byproduct of what those partners send back.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] Treat the database as a referral source for wealth partners (planners, accountants, insurance), not your own pipeline. Each client yields three to four referrals.
- [POST-FUND] BRX concierge callers phone each client, onboard them to a monthly home equity email, then ask who handles their planning and taxes. Gaps become referrals.
- [REFER] Before handing a lead over, run a business conversation: ideal client, top referral source, revenue per transaction, then ask for a reciprocity commitment.
- [REFER] End of week, call the partner, confirm the leads were reached, then ask who they are working with now.
- [POST-FUND] Cadence: engagement ramp-up, a seven day call after closing, a thirty day check-in, then six month and one year mortgage efficiency checkups.
- [MODEL] Partners can give leads, introductions to other professionals, and their database. Adopting partner databases is how the Texas case markets far beyond his own clients.

## Gates
- No business conversation, no leads handed over.
- No stated ideal client, no lead sent, since the wrong lead wastes the partner.
- No reciprocity commitment asked for, no business back.
- No database at scale, no shift, new agents build the base first.

## Numbers
400 clients conservatively yields 1,200 referrals [implied]. One BRX agent got 18 referrals in a week for wealth partners, another four leads in a week [stated, self-reported by the brokerage founder and coach]. A Texas broker markets to 30,000 monthly off 4,000 past clients [stated].

## Delegation map
Concierge callers, including Peckford's son and his daughter's boyfriend, run the onboarding and question calls. The principal owns the partner business conversation and the accountability call. No principal minutes stated.

## Solo translation
A solo agent can run this. The concierge caller is the replaceable role: automation sends the monthly report, queues the seven day, thirty day, six month and one year touches, and drafts the partner scorecard. The partner business conversation and the weekly ask must stay human.

## Tensions
Directly against the advice to build 250 clients and market to them, and against renewal retention as the growth engine, since lender competition has killed winning renewals on rate. Also against polite give and hope etiquette. Note the tool driving these calls sponsors the show.
