# M-17: Jolene Cloutier, 260 files then the rebuild
source: What_Scaling_to_260_Deals_Taught_Her_About_Burning_Out_and_O_mp3.txt
context: Transcript spells her Jolene Cloutier, not Joline. Canadian, northern New Brunswick, peaked at 260 plus files in a year with her husband, now running it with one assistant. Ownwell sponsor, incentive noted.

## Big idea
She scaled to 260 files on hustle and a spreadsheet, and the rebuild now aims at a business she does not need a break from.

## Journey practices
- [LEAD] Anyone reaching out by phone, DM, email or text gets the same apply link, so every lead lands in one place.
- [INTAKE] Clients book their own slot on her calendar. Ranking a call list herself drained her energy and stalled callbacks for days.
- [INTAKE] In 2021 she paid someone to build the inquiry to pre-approval process, then tweaked it to find the bottleneck. Her biggest change.
- [MODEL] The brokerage move became one reset, decided in two weeks: Filogix to Finmo, Outlook to Gmail. Finmo writes her submission notes.
- [COMMIT-TO-FUND] She is building workflows in My Broker Pro to automate the repeated status emails to client, lawyer and realtor.
- [REFER] The podcast, July 2023, is deliberately not about mortgages. Guests share it, some became clients, referrals follow. She runs it in seasons.

## Gates
- No apply link, no file, whatever channel the lead used.
- No booked slot, no call. She will not rank a call list by hand.
- No single channel, no clean file. A text change never reaches fulfillment.
- No prompt, no post. She keeps no content list.

## Delegation map
She personally takes intake calls and client conversations. One assistant runs fulfillment and much else. Her husband left the files in 2021 for the apartment business. No principal minutes stated.

## Solo translation
A solo agent can copy most of this. The apply link and self-booked calendar replace an intake coordinator, and the status emails to client, lawyer and realtor are the fulfillment role's main output. The intake call and catching an idea the moment it arrives stay human.

## Tensions
She reached 260 files before building any system, the reverse of systems-first advice, and Atkin was told to buy an underwriter where she bought a process builder then software. She also wants fewer deals, not more, and refuses the content calendar most advice demands.
