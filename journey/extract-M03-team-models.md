# M-03: Celebrity, point guard, or dentist
source: Celebrity__Point_Guard__or_Dentist__What_s_Your_Mortgage_Tea_mp3.txt
context: Peckford solo episode from his $25M blueprint training. Includes an OwnWell sponsor read: monthly home-equity report, high open rate, drives should-I-buy and should-I-refi conversations.

## Big idea
Pick the team model by the skill you refuse to give up. Salespeople should build the point guard model. Celebrity is where everyone starts and where most get stuck.

## Journey practices
- [MODEL] Celebrity: help exists but hides behind the scenes, every conversation still routes to the broker. Ceiling around 100 files a year, no clean vacations. Dustin Woodhouse cited as the extreme case, around $100M with invisible assistants and long days.
- [INTAKE, PLAN] Point guard, his recommendation for 80% of brokers: the broker keeps only the discovery call, then hands off while pumping the team's tires so the client stops wanting the broker afterward. Peter Matheny funds 400-500 files at roughly 45 broker-minutes per client. Renée Huss did 367 files on a 15-20 minute discovery call, a couple of Loom videos, about 45 minutes total per transaction.
- [MODEL] Dentist: broker appears only at the strategy or signing moment, Mark Good style at up to 500 files, or never, Dan Eisner style, a marketing machine with zero client contact. Needs marketing skill plus people management, the rarest pairing, most profitable, hardest to build.

## Gates
None stated. The models are structures, not rules.

## Numbers
~100-file celebrity ceiling [implied]. 367 to 500 files for point guard and dentist examples [stated]. 15-45 broker minutes per file [stated].

## Delegation map
The episode is a delegation map: the design decision is which client-facing minutes the broker keeps.

## Solo translation
Mike runs the celebrity model today by necessity, every conversation routes to him. Point guard with software as the team is the target: he keeps discovery and planning, the same two calls Chad Wilson keeps, and the pump-the-tires handoff becomes a handoff to portal and automation delivered with the same confidence framing. Dentist is the eventual multi-agent platform ambition, level-3 work, not this quarter.

## Tensions
Explicitly ranks against the celebrity structure most brokers default to. The OwnWell segment is sponsor content, incentive noted, though the monthly-equity-report mechanic matches Strategic Mortgage Monitoring closely enough to study on its own merits.
