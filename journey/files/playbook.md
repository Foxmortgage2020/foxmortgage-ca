# The renewal conversation playbook

The standing shape. This does not change per client. The brief changes.

The objective of every drip touch is to book this session. The objective of
this session is not to win a switch. It is to reach the truthful answer fast,
and then to make sure Fox Mortgage is holding the relationship whichever way
the answer lands.

## Open on their number

The client booked because a specific dollar figure landed in front of them.
Open there, out loud, in their words.

Say the number, say where it came from, and ask whether it matches what their
lender has told them. That question does three things at once. It confirms
the math is live, it finds out whether the incumbent has already made an
offer, and it puts the client in the position of comparing rather than being
sold to.

Do not open with rate. Do not open with the market. Open with their money.

## Honest triage first

Before anything else, find out which of three situations this actually is.
Most calls resolve inside ten minutes and the remaining time is spent on the
thing that matters instead of on a pitch.

Ask about position before product:

- What has the incumbent offered, in writing or on the phone
- Has anything changed at home, in income, in the property, in plans
- Is there other debt sitting at a worse rate than the mortgage
- Is anyone thinking about moving, renovating, or helping a kid buy
- Is the payment comfortable or tight

Those five questions decide the branch. They also surface the pain points
that make a restructure worth more than a rate.

## The education frame

The move is from unconsciously incompetent to consciously incompetent. Most
renewing clients believe a mortgage is a rate. They do not know what they do
not know, and the honest service is showing them the shape of the decision
before helping them make it.

This is not a lecture. It is a short reframe, then questions.

The line that does the work: the rate is the price, the terms are what the
thing actually does. A client can win on price and lose badly on terms, and
renewal is the only moment every few years when both are open.

Then walk the five.

## Five things beside rate

Composed set, pending Mike's confirmation. Not quoted from JOURNEY.md.

1. **Amortization.** The lever that moves the payment most and the one clients
   never think to touch at renewal. Shortening it is the quiet wealth move.
   Extending it is the pressure valve when the payment is tight.
2. **Prepayment privileges and the penalty formula.** What they can pay down
   without cost, and what leaving early would actually cost. Interest rate
   differential versus three months interest is the difference between a
   manageable exit and a trapped one.
3. **Portability and assumability.** Whether the mortgage can move with them.
   This is the thing that matters most to the client who mentioned moving in
   the triage questions and did not connect it to their renewal.
4. **Product structure.** Fixed, variable, hybrid, or a readvanceable setup.
   What the structure is for, not which one is winning right now.
5. **Access to equity.** What the property is worth, what is owed, and what
   that gap could do. Renovation, debt consolidation, a down payment for a
   child, or nothing at all, which is a fine answer.

The client does not need to become an expert on all five. They need to know
the five exist and to have an opinion on the two that touch their life.

## The three branches

### Branch A, the switch has value

The math clears and the terms are better. Say so plainly with the number,
then move straight to the gate.

Nothing advances without the application and the documents. Say that as a
service, not a hurdle. FSRA treats a renewal as a fresh transaction, so the
suitability and disclosure work is real work, not paperwork theatre, and it
protects the client.

The status this produces is `Ready To Renew - Sent New Application`. Hand off
to the signing package skill when the commitment arrives.

### Branch B, the incumbent wins

The bank's offer is genuinely competitive, or the penalty to leave eats the
gain, or the client's plans make moving lenders a bad idea.

Say so. Coach them to take it. Tell them what to ask their lender for before
they sign, because a client who negotiates ten more basis points because Mike
told them how is a client who remembers that.

**Then close on the monitoring load, not on the statement.** Do not end the
call on the no. The close is that Fox Mortgage keeps watching the file, so
that the next time something is worth doing, they hear about it with the
number attached instead of finding out three years later.

This is the honest no, and it is the referral engine. It is also the branch
most likely to be undersold, because it does not feel like a win in the
moment. It is the branch that builds the monitored book.

The status is `Renewed With Us` if Mike arranged the renewal with the
existing lender, and `Renewed Elsewhere` only if the client went direct and
Mike had no hand in it. That distinction is load bearing, because
`Renewed With Us` counts as a retention win in the north star benchmark and
`Renewed Elsewhere` does not. Staying with the same lender is not the test.
Whether Mike arranged it is the test.

### Branch C, there is no mortgage to renew

Sold, paid out, discharged, or the client no longer needs financing.

Short call. Congratulate them, find out what changed, and ask whether they
want to stay on monitoring for the property or for a future purchase. The
status is `No Longer Needs Mortgage`.

Do not treat this as a wasted call. It is the branch that most often produces
a referral, because there is nothing to sell and the client can hear it.

## What never happens on this call

- No rate presented as a recommendation without the terms beside it
- No pressure to switch when the incumbent's offer wins
- No commitment made that the file has not earned
- No decline delivered any way but by phone, from Mike
- No number spoken that Mike cannot trace to the client's own file

## What the call feeds

Renewal conversion is measured against contacts made, never list coverage.
The corpus reference is roughly 40 percent of contacts made, and roughly 75
to 80 percent of renewal contacts booking a strategy session. Both read
unmeasured for Fox Mortgage until the measurement plumbing lands. The capture
step is what will make them measurable, which is why it is not optional.
